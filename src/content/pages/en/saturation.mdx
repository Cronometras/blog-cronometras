  <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mb-4 text-gray-800 dark:text-white">What is the Saturation Study Module in CRONOMETRAS?</h2>

  <p class="mb-6 text-gray-700 dark:text-gray-300">
    The <strong>Saturation Study Module</strong> is a specialized tool in <a href="/en/features" class="text-accent hover:underline">CRONOMETRAS</a> designed to measure, in a single session, the <strong>real saturation percentage of the operator and the machine</strong> during a production process. While the <a href="/en/blog/saturation-calculation-in-time-study" class="text-accent hover:underline">theoretical saturation calculation</a> is derived from a classical time study, this module lets you record <strong>what happens at every moment</strong>: when the machine is running and when it is stopped, when the operator is working and when they are waiting, through two independent stopwatches with start and stop buttons.
  </p>

  <p class="mb-6 text-gray-700 dark:text-gray-300">
    The result is a <strong>saturation % calculated live</strong> from the actual measured segments, without prior estimates. This gives you an objective basis for decisions on <a href="/en/blog/calculating-the-optimal-number-of-machines-for-an-operator" class="text-accent hover:underline">machine-to-operator assignment</a>, line balancing and production planning.
  </p>
