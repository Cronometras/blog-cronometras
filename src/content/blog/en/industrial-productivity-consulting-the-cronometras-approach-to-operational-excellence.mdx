# Industrial Productivity Consulting: The Cronometras Approach to Operational Excellence

In a saturated market, standard management consulting often stays at the KPI and Powerpoint level. The **Cronometras** approach is different: we descend to the shop floor physics. We understand that **Operational Excellence** is not a slogan, but the mathematical result of eliminating friction, variability, and waste (Muda) from the value chain.

Our consulting model is simple: we don't assume; we measure. Starting from a video of your operation (or direct measurement on site), we break the work down into elements, measure and rate the times, apply the OIT and TAL supplements, and build the standard time on which you can plan and decide.

## The 4 Pillars of Our Methodology

### 1. Master Data Integrity Audit
Before optimizing, we must validate. Routing times in ERP master data are often outdated. We audit your existing standards - their variability and obsolescence - and establish standards that reflect the true capacity of your installed assets.

### 2. Ergonomics as a Productive Strategy
Work pace and allowances must respect the worker's physical limits. A physically sustainable process is a stable process. We account for fatigue and rest needs when building the standard, so the OEE curve stays stable throughout the shift.

### 3. Digitalization of the Method
We transform tribal knowledge into documented assets: the defined method, its work elements and its standard time, in a report in PDF, Excel and JSON that your team can keep using and updating.

### 4. Dynamic Management Control
We help your team transition from "Reactive Firefighting" to "Predictive Control", training your internal team so the standards stay alive and decisions are fed by data, not intuition.
